A Letter For Doaa

From the heart — just words that mean something.

My dearest Doaa,

I don't know if words will ever be enough to explain what you mean to me, but I want to try. Since you became a part of my life, ordinary days have started to feel different. Your name can change my mood, your presence can calm my heart, and even the smallest moment with you can become a memory I want to keep forever.

I love you not only for the beautiful moments, but also for the quiet ones — the conversations that seem to have no ending, the little jokes, the worries we share, and the simple feeling that I am not alone when you are beside me. You became someone I look for in my happiest moments and someone I want near me when life gets difficult.

I cannot promise that every day will be perfect. Life will have difficult days, misunderstandings, pressure, and moments when we need patience with each other. But I can promise that my feelings are real, and that I will always want to choose you, understand you, protect the trust between us, and work for the beautiful future we dream about together.

Thank you for every smile you have given me, every conversation, every memory, and every second that made me feel lucky to know you. If I could keep one thing forever, it would be the feeling of having you in my life. And if I could write one sentence over and over again, it would simply be: I love you, Doaa, more than I know how to put into words.

Whatever the future brings, I hope we keep growing together, laughing together, supporting each other, and turning ordinary days into memories worth remembering. You are not just a chapter in my story — you are one of the reasons I want the story to keep going.

Forever yours,
Abdelaleem ♥
